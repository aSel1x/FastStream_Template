# Code of conduct

## In short

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that makes this project better: giving specific, actionable feedback;
saying "I don't know"; accepting that a maintainer may decline a change and explaining your
reasoning once rather than repeatedly.

Examples of behaviour that will not be tolerated: harassment of any kind, personal or political
attacks, publishing others' private information, sexualised language or imagery, and sustained
disruption of discussion.

## Scope

Applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, and to
anyone representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a private security advisory or contacting the
maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit, remove or reject
contributions that violate this code, and may ban a contributor for repeated or severe
violations.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
